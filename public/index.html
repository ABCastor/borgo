The best place to live, for you

Borgo is yours. It ranks cities on what you value: what you keep after tax and rent, days that feel the way you like, where you have the right to live and work. Every number shows its source, and nothing you enter leaves your browser.

Browse the sources, a city such as Lisbon or Berlin, a comparison such as Amsterdam vs Berlin, or the guide for AI assistants; the same engine answers from the command line and as an MCP server. The code is open at github.com/ABCastor/borgo.
