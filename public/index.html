Compare cities by what you value

Borgo compares any number of cities on open data, weighted by what matters to you: what you would earn after tax and keep after rent, who in your household can move there without a visa, how many nice days you get in your own temperature range, air, safety and health. Every figure shows its source and the period it describes. It runs in your browser with no account, and the same engine answers from the command line and as an MCP server for AI assistants.

Browse the sources, a city such as Lisbon or Berlin, a comparison such as Amsterdam vs Berlin, or the guide for AI assistants. The code is open at github.com/ABCastor/borgo.
