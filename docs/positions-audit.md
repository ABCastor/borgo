# Position lines: source audit

Reference date **5 November 2026**. Every legal and political fact behind the three position lines (politics, abortion
law, same-sex couples in law) was compared with its primary source: the statute, an official government page that
states it, or the electoral authority's official result. A fact counts as checked only when the source's own text was
read; a cookie wall, a JavaScript shell, a blocked page or a search excerpt is not a check. A fact that could not be
checked is either stated from the law cited and marked so in its note, or removed where it was doubtful.

**Five Star Movement.** ParlGov 2024 gives it no left-right figure (party 2155, "no family"). It stays unpositioned, its
15.4% stays in the denominator and in the note, and it is never put at the midpoint. With Azione also unplaced, Italy
is under the 75% rule and has no figure. Placing M5S needs an expert source that covers it; the Chapel Hill Expert
Survey does, and its terms of reuse decide whether it can be added.

**Family means.** ParlGov gives a party no expert survey covers its family's mean, and many large parties rest on it:
AfD, Sweden Democrats, Chega, Brothers of Italy, Reform UK and JA21 at 8.7; ANO, NEOS, Estonia 200 and Liberal Alliance
at 6.0. The note marks these "party-family average". Two family means are plainly off for the party: the Swiss Green
Liberals at the green family's 2.5 and Katter's Australian Party at the social-democratic family's 3.3. They are
ParlGov's published figures and stay, marked, until an expert source covers those parties.

## Wording

- The "Where you stand" lede: "How each place voted, as an estimate from left to right, and what its law allows, each
  with its source. Say where you stand and a place scores by how close it is; nothing is assumed about you."
- The politics note opens "Estimate from the official result of …", says "no position" for unplaced parties and marks
  party-family and members' averages.
- The politics words name fixed points on the scale; they do not classify a place or its voters.
- Couples steps: 0 "Criminalised" means same-sex relations are a crime; 1 is "Legal, no union in law".
- Law texts state the statutory grounds and conditions in the law's own terms, without characterising how they are
  applied; open questions are not left in user-facing notes.
- The metric, MCP and agent descriptions call the politics figure an estimate and name the places without one.
